Our Story

Fève de Cacao was born from a love of fine chocolate and a commitment to ethical sourcing. We work directly with cacao farmers to bring you the highest quality beans, transformed into exceptional products in our workshop.

Every bar, every nib, every cup tells a story — from the farmers who nurture the cacao trees to the careful roasting and refining that brings out the unique flavors of each origin.
